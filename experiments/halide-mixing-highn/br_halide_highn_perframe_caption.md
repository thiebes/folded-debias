# Caption -- br_halide_highn_perframe.png

**Figure N.** Per-frame fitted variance sigma^2(t) on the high-statistics
(400,000-walker) Br-I halide-mixing maps at initial contrast-to-noise CNR_0 = 10,
for Br fractions (a) 0.25, (b) 0.5, (c) 0.75. Points are the mean over 300 noisy
realizations; the indigo line is the carrier transport ground truth. The naive
width fit (red) lifts above truth as the per-frame contrast falls -- the upward
truncation bias -- while the folded-normal correction (blue) recovers the truth
slope. The shaded band marks frames below the per-frame CNR floor of 1.3, excluded
from the diffusion-coefficient fit; it begins earliest in (a) because the
least-disordered, fastest-spreading map dims soonest. Over the usable (unshaded)
window the naive fit inflates the recovered coefficient to D_naive/D_true =
1.13 / 1.19 / 1.22 for (a)/(b)/(c), and the correction brings it to D_corr/D_true =
1.03 / 0.96 / 1.06.
