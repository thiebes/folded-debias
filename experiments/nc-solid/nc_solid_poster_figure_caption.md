**On a nanocrystal-solid transport bed, noise pushes the reported exponent toward
ballistic — and the correction pulls it back.** Apparent MSD exponent α against
CNR₀ for a realistic-width bed built from Solari et al.'s exciton-hopping map (the
C16 bed; nominal α = 0.883, gold line). The naive fit (red) inflates α as contrast
falls; the corrected fit (blue) holds near nominal across the measured regime. The
inset extends to low contrast, where the naive exponent saturates at the ballistic
ceiling (α = 2). White measurement noise is scaled to each CNR₀, and α is read from
the MSD averaged over 64,000 independent noise realisations, with a bootstrap
standard error. Bed: 250 × 250 spatial bins, 20 frames, exciton lifetime τ = 7 ns.
