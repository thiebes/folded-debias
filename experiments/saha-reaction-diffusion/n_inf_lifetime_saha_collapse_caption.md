The exact Saha proportionality behind the rho-nu degeneracy. The Saha free-charge
fraction satisfies (1-x) = x^2 n/A exactly, so the exciton per-carrier rate
nu(1-x) = nu*x^2*n/A is proportional to the bimolecular per-carrier rate
rho*x^2*n at all densities. Left: the two per-carrier rates versus density
(parallel on the log axes). Right: their ratio, flat at nu/(rho*A) across nine
decades (deviation 6e-14). Dotted: n = A; light lines: the three measured
fluences. The fit sees only the sum nu + rho*A, which is why rho is degenerate.
