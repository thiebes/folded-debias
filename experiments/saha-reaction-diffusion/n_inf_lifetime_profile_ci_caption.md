Profile likelihood of each rate: fix one, re-fit the rest, and read
SSR - SSR_min against the fixed value. nu is determined as a bracket (a plateau
with a hard lower wall, not a point); lam is a clean well; gamma is an interior
minimum near 1e8 s^-1; rho is flat and is not determined by the lifetime data.
Dotted: the s^2 threshold (~1 sigma); dashed: the global best fit. The profile
intervals assume independent residuals, so the formal brackets (lam's most) are
narrower than the true intervals.
