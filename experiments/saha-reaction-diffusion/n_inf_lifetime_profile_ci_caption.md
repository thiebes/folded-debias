Profile likelihood of each rate: fix one, re-fit the rest, and read
SSR - SSR_min against the fixed value. nu is determined as a bracket (a plateau
with a hard lower wall, not a point); lambda is a clean well (4.06e8 s^-1, formal
1-sigma [3.96, 4.17]e8); gamma is an interior minimum near 1e8 s^-1; rho is flat
and is not determined by the lifetime data. Dotted: the s^2 threshold (~1 sigma);
dashed: the global best fit. The profile intervals assume independent residuals,
so the formal brackets (lambda's most) are narrower than the true intervals.
