Global multi-fluence fit of the three n_inf PL decays with one shared rate set
(nu, rho, lam, gamma, qtrap; only Nexc differs between curves). Points are the
data, lines the fit. The shared-rate model reproduces the overall decay shape
across 0.01-20 ns and the density ordering; it under-fits the late-time tail at
the two lower fluences, which bears on the trap parameters, not on the
identifiability result below.
