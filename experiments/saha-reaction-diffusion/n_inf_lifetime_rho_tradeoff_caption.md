The Saha rho-nu degeneracy made visible. Profiling rho while re-fitting nu: the
re-fit nu (left, blue) tracks nu_eff - rho*A (red dashed) to four significant
figures, holding nu + rho*A fixed, while the fit quality (right) stays flat --
until rho*A reaches nu (green line, ~1.5e-9), where nu can no longer compensate
(it pins to its floor) and the fit breaks. The lifetime data determine only the
sum nu + rho*A, not rho on its own, because at n >= A the Saha free-charge
fraction makes the bimolecular rate scale as rho*A, the same density form as nu.
