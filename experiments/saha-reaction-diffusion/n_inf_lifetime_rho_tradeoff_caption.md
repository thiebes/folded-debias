The Saha rho-nu degeneracy made visible. Profiling rho while re-fitting the rest.
Left: the fit quality (SSR) stays flat until rho*A reaches nu (dotted, ~1.5e-9),
where nu can no longer compensate and the fit breaks. Right: the re-fit nu (blue)
tracks nu_eff - rho*A (dashed), while lam (green) and gamma (magenta) do not move
at all -- only nu trades against rho. The lifetime data determine only the sum
nu + rho*A, not rho on its own. The degeneracy is exact: the Saha identity
(1-x) = x^2 n/A makes the exciton rate nu(1-x) exactly proportional to the
bimolecular rate rho*x^2*n at every density (see the saha_collapse figure).
