# Caption -- measurement schematic (poster Panel 1)

The measurement. Transient-PL / stroboSCAT microscopy tracks a spreading, dimming
excited-state distribution; the width of each frame gives the mean-squared
displacement, hence the diffusion coefficient D (and, in disordered films, a
subdiffusive exponent alpha). The per-frame contrast-to-noise ratio falls frame by
frame as the population spreads (broadening) and recombines (dimming): early frames
are narrow and bright (high CNR), late frames wide and dim (low CNR). The hook: noise
sets both how precisely and how accurately D can be measured, yet the per-frame CNR
is almost never reported.

Pure schematic -- the only physical curve is the Gaussian profile from the
simulation model; everything else is annotation.
